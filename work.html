Work

Campaign design and event branding for UBC eProjects, alongside my visual art practice. Full case studies below — process included where it exists, not just finished posters.

UBC eProjects

Launch Party

eProjects' first event of the year, built around a launch-to-space concept — the idea being that the club's ideas would be "launched" now and explored further as the year went on.

This became the club's first event to draw real buzz and attention on campus.

Process: hand-drawn sketch → digitally painted concept → final flat vector design, adapted for both a portrait poster and a wide LinkedIn/banner crop.
UBC eProjects — Flagship Event

Executive Lounge

eProjects' flagship event, bringing in 20 delegates to share their experience in their roles and as founders of their own companies. I designed the full branded guidebook, from cover to logistics.

The most ambitious piece of event branding I've done for the club — a multi-page guidebook rather than a single poster.

UBC eProjects

Accelerate

An event connecting students directly with startup accelerators — built on the idea that once ideas are formed, they need to be accelerated. I followed the initial poster with a "24 hours left" urgency post to drive last-minute turnout.

A two-part push: an announcement poster, then a countdown reminder closer to the date.

Process: the poster's color story started as an ideation/gradient study before landing on the final blue-to-red palette.
UBC eProjects — Brand Refresh

eBulb Mascot Revamp

eProjects' mascot, eBulb, needed to be modernized to match the club's updated "e" lightbulb logo. I worked through a systematic ideation process — color, eyes, blush, and base — before landing on the final design.

A deliberate design-system approach rather than a single redraw: every variable was tested before the final mascot was locked in.

Process: old mascot → build-your-own ideation sheet (color, eyes, blush, base options tested systematically) → final revamped mascot matching the modern logo.
UBC eProjects — Brand Identity

Sticker Series

A set of five digital stickers designed to capture the club's energy and constant innovation — artistic and expressive rather than a plain brand logo.

Drove higher visibility and curiosity — first-year students asked about the club specifically because of the stickers.
